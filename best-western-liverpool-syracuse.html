The short version

You found the right hotel.

If you searched "Best Western Liverpool NY" and ended up here — yes, this is the right place. The hotel at 136 Transistor Pkwy in Liverpool, just off I-90 next to Lockheed Martin, is open and operating. In the last couple of years we left the Best Western Plus brand and reopened as the Syracuse Grand: an independent, locally-run hotel.

Same building. Same front desk team a lot of you already know. Same indoor pool, same hot breakfast in the morning, same free parking out front. What changed is the name on the sign and the website you book through — which is now syracusegrand.com.

Why we rebranded

More flexibility, better stays.

Going independent let us do a few things the brand structure didn't make easy:

  • Better direct rates. No franchise fee built into every nightly rate means we can match or beat what you'd see on the booking sites — without you giving up 15% to a middleman.
  • Local packages. SU game day weekends, NYS Fair stays, Empower FCU Amphitheater concert nights, Micron contractor rates — we can put those together without waiting on corporate approval.
  • Faster room upgrades. We're putting money back into the building on our own timeline instead of a brand-mandated PIP cycle.
  • Real people answering the phone. Call (315) 701-4400 and you get the front desk in Liverpool, not a call center in another state.

None of that changes the basics. Two-queen rooms, king rooms, and king suites. Indoor heated pool and hot tub, open daily. Free hot breakfast — eggs, waffles, breakfast meats, fruit, yogurt — every morning. Free parking, free Wi-Fi, pet-friendly with a small pet fee. 24-hour fitness center. Same as before.

FAQ

The questions we get a lot.

Is the Best Western in Liverpool, NY still open?

Yes. It's open and operating as the Syracuse Grand — same building, same address (136 Transistor Pkwy), same local team. Just a new name on the sign and a new website.

What happened to the Best Western Plus?

We left the brand and went independent. The franchise model worked for a long time; eventually the math and the flexibility tipped the other way. Same hotel, just no longer flying the Best Western flag.

Can I still use Best Western Rewards points here?

No — the property is independent now. The trade-off is direct-booking rates without a franchise fee baked in. Book at syracusegrand.com for the best price, or call (315) 701-4400 and we'll match.

Did anything actually change at the hotel?

Same amenities, same staff, same layout. We're continuing to upgrade rooms and common areas on our own schedule.

How do I book?

Three ways: book direct at syracusegrand.com, call the front desk at (315) 701-4400, or email us through the contact page. For group blocks or weddings, our groups team handles those directly.
