The Micron Clay Project

Micron Technology's semiconductor fabrication campus in Clay, NY is one of the largest domestic manufacturing investments in U.S. history — a $100 billion commitment over 20 years that will eventually employ approximately 9,000 direct workers and tens of thousands more in the supply chain and construction trades. The multi-fab campus will build leading-edge DRAM chips, with the first fab scheduled to come online in the early 2030s.

That timeline means the project is in an extended construction and infrastructure phase right now, with engineers, project managers, specialty contractors, environmental consultants, and equipment vendors cycling through the region for rotations ranging from a few days to several months. If that's you, this guide is for you.

The Route to the Micron Clay Site

From the hotel, the route is straightforward:

  1. Take Electronics Pkwy north to the I-90 on-ramp heading west.
  2. Take I-90 West to Exit 36 (Weedsport / NY-34).
  3. Follow NY-31 east toward Clay — the site access points will be signed as the project progresses.

Total drive time is approximately 10 minutes in normal conditions. Allow extra time during shift-change windows as the project ramps up and worker volumes increase on NY-31 in the morning.
