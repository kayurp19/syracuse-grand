Your guide to airport hotels near SYR

February 14, 2026 · 4 min read

Syracuse Hancock International Airport (SYR) is small, friendly, and easy to navigate — but if you have an early flight, a late arrival, or a layover with family in tow, picking the right hotel makes a big difference. Here's what to look for.

What matters most in an airport hotel

1. Drive time, not just distance

SYR is north of downtown, off I-81 and I-481. The closest hotels are within 5 miles. We're at 4 miles — about 8 minutes door-to-curbside in normal traffic.

2. Free parking, especially long-term

If you're flying out and back, paid airport parking adds up fast. Hotels like ours offer free on-site parking, and many travelers leave their car with us during their trip.

3. 24-hour front desk

Late flights happen. So do delays. A staffed front desk at any hour means you'll never arrive to a locked lobby.

4. Hot breakfast included

Especially helpful for early departures — grab coffee and a real meal before heading to TSA.

5. Quiet rooms

Look for hotels with newer windows, well-insulated walls, and rooms positioned away from highway noise. Real reviews from business travelers are the best indicator.
