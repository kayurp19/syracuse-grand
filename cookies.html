Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies and similar technologies are used on syracusegrand.com (the "Site"), operated by Kevidco LLC d/b/a Syracuse Grand. It works alongside our Privacy Policy.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make sites work, to remember your preferences, and to provide information to the site owner. We also use similar technologies — pixels, tags, and local storage — and we refer to all of them collectively as "cookies" in this policy.

How you control cookies on this Site

When you first visit the Site, our cookie banner asks you to choose:

  • Accept All: allow Analytics and Functional services. Advertising remains off.
  • Reject All: keep optional Analytics and the embedded map off.
  • Manage Preferences: turn Analytics and Functional services on or off individually.

You can change your choices any time by clicking Cookie Settings at the bottom of any page.

You can also disable or delete cookies and site storage through your browser settings. If storage is blocked, we cannot remember your choice between pages. The separate reservation website has its own storage requirements and controls.

Where supported, we honor browser-level opt-out signals such as the Global Privacy Control (GPC).

Categories of cookies we use

1. Strictly Necessary (always on)

We store your cookie preference and the dismissal of site notices on your device. Our hosting infrastructure also processes requests to deliver and secure the Site. Optional analytics are not needed to navigate the Site, submit an inquiry, or open the booking page.

  • sg_cookie_consent: your current categories, action, receipt ID, timestamp, policy version and expiry; kept in browser local storage for up to 180 days.
  • sg_cookie_consent_history: up to 20 unexpired consent receipts, stored only in this browser.
  • sg_banner_dismissed: remembers dismissal of the hotel notice for the browser session.

2. Functional

The Google Maps embed on our Contact page loads only after you allow Functional services or select “Enable Google Map.” Loading it sends your IP address and browser information to Google, which may use its own cookies or storage. You can instead follow the external directions link without enabling the embedded map on this Site. External Google pages have their own privacy settings.

3. Analytics & performance

Help us understand how visitors find and use the Site so we can improve it. With your permission, we use:

  • Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC), measurement ID G-9PGM0017B6: page visits, traffic sources, device information, booking-link clicks, telephone/email-link clicks and successful contact-form events. Form message contents are not sent as analytics event parameters.
  • GA cookies include _ga and _ga_9PGM0017B6. We configure a 180-day lifetime, which can renew with activity while consent remains valid.

4. Advertising

No Google Ads conversion tag, Meta Pixel, or Microsoft Bing UET tag is currently installed on this Site. Advertising storage, advertising user data and ad personalization remain denied. Google Signals and ad-personalization signals are disabled in our Analytics configuration. The advertising category is shown as “Not in use”; accepting optional services does not enable it.

Google Consent Mode v2

We use a basic opt-in implementation with Google Consent Mode v2. Consent defaults are set in the page head. The Google Analytics library is not loaded and no Analytics requests are sent until you allow Analytics. Rejecting, dismissing the notice, or leaving it unanswered keeps Analytics off. Withdrawing Analytics consent clears accessible first-party Analytics cookies and reloads the page to stop the previously loaded library. This does not undo data already sent while consent was active.

Other services and external links

Google Fonts supplies the site's typefaces and receives the network information needed to deliver font files; it is not loaded as an analytics tag. Railway hosts the website. Contact inquiries go through our website server to the hotel's configured email-delivery service (Resend when enabled, or its SMTP provider). Booking links open Vertical Booking on a separate website. These services are described in our Privacy Policy service inventory; this Site's controls do not manage cookies on external booking, maps or social-media websites.

How long cookies last

Your choice expires after 180 days. We ask again if it expires, if you clear browser storage, or if the consent-policy version changes. Expired receipts are excluded when records are read or updated. You can reopen Cookie Settings to see the current timestamp and download the current receipt and available history. This is a browser-local record, not a central or independently verified consent audit log. Clearing browser storage removes it; if storage is unavailable, your choice applies only to the current page.

Updates to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent change.
