House Policies & Hotel Hours

Daily Schedule

  • Check-out: 11:00 AM. Late check-out by request, subject to availability.
  • Express check-out: Drop your key in the front-desk box on your way out — your folio will be emailed.
  • Breakfast: Mon–Fri 6:00–9:00 AM · Sat–Sun 7:00–10:00 AM, in the breakfast room off the lobby.
  • Fitness center: Open 24 hours with your room key.
  • Quiet hours: 9:00 PM Sun–Fri · 10:00 PM Saturday.

Housekeeping

Daily refresh on request. Full housekeeping service is provided every 2–3 days for stays of multiple nights — call the front desk at any time if you need fresh towels, linens, or amenities sooner.

Occupancy

Each room is priced for two guests. Additional guests after the first two are $10 per person, per night. Maximum occupancy is set per room type — please ask the front desk before adding extra guests.

Smoke-Free Policy

The Syracuse Grand is 100% smoke-free — including all rooms, balconies, and indoor common areas. This applies to cigarettes, cigars, vapes, e-cigarettes, marijuana, incense, and candles. A $250 cleaning fee is charged to any room found in violation.

Other Rules

  • No parties or large gatherings in guest rooms.
  • Drug-free property. Underage alcohol consumption is not permitted.
  • Sporting equipment, weapons, and similar items must be stored in your vehicle.
  • Pets welcome in designated rooms with a non-refundable pet fee — please notify the front desk at check-in.
  • No refunds after check-in. Cancellation policies follow your booking source.
Pool & Hot Tub Rules

Hours & Capacity

  • Open daily: 9:00 AM – 10:00 PM
  • Maximum capacity: 31 guests total — 26 in the pool, 5 in the hot tub.
  • Pool and spa are unsupervised. Swim at your own risk.

Safety

  • Never swim alone. Always have someone with you.
  • Children under 16 must be supervised by an adult at all times.
  • No running, diving, or rough play in the pool area.
  • Shower before entering the pool or spa.

Hot Tub

  • Maximum 7 people at a time.
  • 15-minute limit per session.
  • Children under 16 are not permitted in the hot tub without direct adult supervision.
  • Pregnant women, anyone with heart conditions or high blood pressure, and guests who have consumed alcohol should not use the hot tub.

In an emergency, dial 9-1-1, then call the front desk by dialing 0 from your room phone (or (315) 701-4400 from a cell).

Emergency & Floor Plan

In any emergency, dial 9-1-1 first, then call the front desk by dialing 0 from your room phone (or (315) 701-4400 from a cell). Please take a moment to locate the nearest exit, fire pull station, and extinguisher to your room.

Floor Plan — Both Floors

First floor: rooms 101–129. Second floor: rooms 201–234.

If You Hear the Alarm

  • Feel the door before opening — if it's hot, do not open. Stay in your room, seal the door with wet towels, and signal from the window.
  • If the door is cool, exit using the nearest marked stairwell — never use the elevator.
  • Stay low if there is smoke. Walk, don't run.
  • Once outside, gather at the meeting point in the front parking lot, well away from the building.
  • Do not re-enter the building until cleared by the fire department.

Reporting an Emergency

  • Medical / fire / police: 9-1-1
  • Front desk (24 hours): Dial 0 from your room phone, or (315) 701-4400 from a cell
  • Liverpool Fire Department (non-emergency): (315) 457-4030
