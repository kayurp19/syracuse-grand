Why North Syracuse / Cicero / Liverpool

Quieter, faster, easier — at a better rate.

Downtown Syracuse hotels run $50–80 more per night than equivalent rooms north of the city — and you'll still pay $20–25 a night for parking. The Syracuse Grand sits just off I-81 in Liverpool / North Syracuse, eight minutes from downtown via the highway with free parking right outside your room.

For most Syracuse trips, north-side hotels make more sense. You're closer to Syracuse Hancock Airport (4 miles), the NYS Fairgrounds (7 miles), Destiny USA (5 miles), Onondaga Lake Park (5 minutes), and the entire I-81 / I-690 / I-90 corridor — all without sitting in downtown traffic at rush hour.

The neighborhoods around us — Liverpool village, Cicero, North Syracuse, Salina — are quieter, residential, and the kind of places you can actually drive around without one-way streets every block. We're a short walk from local diners, breweries, and the Onondaga Lake waterfront.

What's around the hotel

The North Syracuse / Cicero / Liverpool area.

More to do here than people realize. Less hassle than downtown.

Within 5–10 minutes

  • Onondaga Lake Park: Seven-mile paved loop trail, dog-friendly, fishing piers, picnic areas. Free.
  • Heid's of Liverpool: Iconic Syracuse-style hot dog and Coney Island stand. (Note: closed in 2024 — check current status before going.)
  • Wegmans (Liverpool): Five minutes — pharmacy, prepared meals, café, late-night hot food.
  • Liverpool village: Walkable historic district, restaurants, ice cream, antique shops.
  • The Salt Museum: Free historical museum on Onondaga Lake, telling the story of Syracuse's salt industry.

Within 15 minutes

  • Syracuse Hancock Airport (SYR): 4 miles, 7 minutes — one of the closer airport bases.
  • Destiny USA: 5 miles, 10 minutes — sixth-largest mall in the U.S., outlets, entertainment.
  • Downtown Syracuse: 8 minutes via I-81 — Armory Square restaurants, Marriott, Landmark Theatre.
  • Syracuse University and JMA Dome: 6 miles, 12 minutes.
  • Empower FCU Amphitheater: 5 miles, 12 minutes.

Within 30 minutes

  • Micron campus in Clay: 6 miles north — closest hotel base for the megafab project.
  • Skaneateles Lake: 25 minutes — picturesque village, lakefront restaurants.
  • Beak & Skiff Apple Orchards: 30 minutes — apple picking, bakery, distillery.
  • Turning Stone Resort Casino: 30 minutes — casino, golf, dining, full resort.

FAQ

Common questions about North Syracuse stays.

Is Syracuse Grand in Cicero, NY?

Technically Liverpool / North Syracuse — but we're closer to Cicero than most downtown hotels and easily serve travelers visiting Cicero, North Syracuse, Clay, Salina, Liverpool, and the entire north-side area.

What's the difference between Cicero, North Syracuse, and Liverpool?

All are north-of-Syracuse communities sharing the I-81 corridor. Liverpool is closer to Onondaga Lake (where we are), North Syracuse is east, and Cicero is further north toward the Micron site. All are within 5-15 minutes of the hotel.

How far is Syracuse Grand from downtown Syracuse?

About 5 miles — roughly an 8-minute drive via I-81 South. Closer to downtown than most North Syracuse motels.

Why stay north of Syracuse instead of downtown?

Lower rates, free parking, easier highway access to the airport, fairgrounds, Destiny USA, and Micron — and quieter overnight. Downtown makes sense if you specifically need to walk to Armory Square restaurants; for most other trips, the north side is the smarter choice.

Are there other hotels in Cicero, NY I should consider?

There are several budget motels along Route 11 in Cicero. The Syracuse Grand offers full-service amenities (indoor pool, hot tub, full breakfast, business center, fitness center, 24/7 front desk) at a competitive rate.
