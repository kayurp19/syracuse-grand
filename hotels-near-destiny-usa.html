Close enough, far enough

A smart base for a Destiny USA weekend.

Destiny USA is enormous — over 250 stores, multiple restaurants, an indoor go-kart track, mini golf, an arcade, a movie theater, an escape room, and a ropes course. It's a full day, sometimes two. The Syracuse Grand is the kind of hotel that makes that easy: ten minutes away by car, with rooms big enough for the kids, a pool to wear them out at the end of the day, and breakfast included before you head out.

We're set in the village of Liverpool, NY — north of the lake, off Interstate 90 — which means parking is plentiful and free, the area is quiet at night, and you're close to other things to do (Onondaga Lake Park is a mile away, the zoo is across the lake, downtown Syracuse is fifteen minutes). It's a much better setup than parking lot hotels right next to the mall.

From the hotel to the mall

Getting there and what to do.

Driving directions

  • From 136 Transistor Pkwy, head south on Electronics Pkwy.
  • Merge onto I-81 South.
  • Take exit 23 (Hiawatha Blvd) toward Destiny USA.
  • Total: about 5 miles, 10 minutes in normal traffic.

Top picks at Destiny USA (besides shopping)

  • WonderWorks: An indoor amusement park with a 6-story ropes course, a glow-in-the-dark mini-golf, and dozens of hands-on exhibits.
  • Pole Position Raceway: Indoor electric go-karts that rip — adult and kid leagues. Worth the drive.
  • Margaritaville & Toby Keith's: Two of the larger sit-down restaurants, plus dozens more in the food court.
  • 5 Wits: Walk-through adventure rooms — somewhere between an escape room and an immersive movie set.
  • Apex Entertainment: Bowling, billiards, ax throwing, and arcade games under one roof.

For a longer guide, our team wrote up 12 things to do near Destiny USA.

Other nearby fun

  • Onondaga Lake Park (~1 mile): The "Central Park of Central New York" — 8+ miles of paved trail, the Wegmans Boundless Playground, the Salt Museum, and Heid's of Liverpool.
  • Rosamond Gifford Zoo (~5 miles): Across the lake — small, well-run, and worth a half-day.
  • Empower FCU Amphitheater at Lakeview (~4 miles): The lakeside concert venue. Big-name summer tours.
