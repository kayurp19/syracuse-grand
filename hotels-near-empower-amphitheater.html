For the 2026 concert season

Twelve minutes to the venue. Sleep is twelve minutes after the encore.

The Empower FCU Amphitheater at Lakeview on Onondaga Lake is one of the best outdoor concert venues in the Northeast — and 2026 is shaping up to be a packed season. Twenty-plus shows confirmed including Treaty Oak Revival, Kid Cudi, Travis Scott, Stone Temple Pilots, and the venue's 10th-anniversary free community celebration on May 31.

The Syracuse Grand is the smart base for the season. Twelve minutes from the amphitheater via I-690, free self-parking (no $20 venue parking after a long show), late checkout so you can actually sleep in, and a 24/7 front desk that's used to midnight returns from the venue.

Concert-night perks for direct bookers: 1pm late checkout, free hot breakfast 6:30–9:30am, free WiFi for streaming the next day, and a quiet hotel that's set apart from the bar district. Book direct at (315) 701-4400 or check our concert packages.

Driving to the amphitheater

From the hotel to Onondaga Lake.

Twelve minutes via I-690 West — and on the way home post-show, you'll have free parking waiting in our lot.

The route

  • From the hotel at 136 Transistor Pkwy, take Electronics Pkwy to I-690 West.
  • Stay on I-690 West for about 4 miles.
  • Take exit 7 (NYS Fairgrounds / State Fair Boulevard).
  • Follow signs to amphitheater parking on Restoration Way.

Allow 12–15 minutes on a normal day. Add 20–30 minutes pre-show as concertgoers arrive.

Pre-show and post-show

  • Dinner before the show: Heid's of Liverpool, Limp Lizard BBQ, and several local options on Old Liverpool Road and NY-31 — easy stops on the way to or from the venue.
  • Late-night food after the show: Wegmans on John Glenn Blvd has hot prepared food until late, and our front desk can point you to options that are still open at midnight.
  • Onondaga Lake Park: Five minutes from the hotel — great for a morning walk before checkout.
