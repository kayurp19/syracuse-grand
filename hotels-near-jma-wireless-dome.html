Driving to the Dome

From the hotel to Comstock Avenue.

Twelve minutes via I-81 South — well-marked Dome parking, but plan ahead for major events.

The route

  • From the hotel at 136 Transistor Pkwy, take Electronics Pkwy to I-90 East.
  • Merge onto I-81 South toward Syracuse / Binghamton.
  • Take exit 18 (Harrison St / Adams St).
  • Follow signs to Comstock Avenue and Dome parking.

Allow 12–15 minutes on a normal day. Add 20–30 minutes before football games and major concerts when traffic builds on Adams Street.

Major Dome events

  • Syracuse Football: 6–7 home Saturdays per season, September–early December. ACC opponents draw the largest out-of-town demand.
  • Syracuse Basketball: November–March. ACC games and major non-conference matchups sell out.
  • Syracuse Lacrosse: Spring season. ACC tournament weekend is huge.
  • Commencement: May 7–10, 2026 — graduation ceremony Sunday May 10 at the Dome.
  • Concerts: Periodically throughout the year. Check livenation.com for current lineup.
  • NCAA Tournaments and Sports Showcases: The Dome regularly hosts neutral-site games and tournaments.

Bus parking and group needs

For visiting teams and large alumni or fan-club groups: we have on-site parking for charter buses and can block adjoining or same-floor rooms. Contact our group desk to set up an athletic-event block.

FAQ

Common questions for Dome visitors.

How far is Syracuse Grand from the JMA Wireless Dome?

About 6 miles — roughly a 12-minute drive via I-81 South to exit 18.

Do you have a shuttle to the Dome?

We don't run a regular shuttle, but Uber/Lyft from the hotel typically runs $15–20 each way. Many guests find it easier than paying $20–40 for Dome parking.

Can I book a block of rooms for an SU game weekend?

Yes. Call (315) 701-4400 to set up a group block for football, basketball, lacrosse, or commencement weekends.

What's late checkout for game days?

1pm checkout is available for direct bookers on game days — mention it at check-in. (Versus standard 11am.)

Is parking really free?

Yes. Free self-parking for all guests, including game-day weekends. No exceptions, no resort fees.
