Why teams pick us

A reliable base for the Micron project.

The new Micron semiconductor fabrication campus in Clay, NY is one of the largest private investments in New York State history. Construction will run for years, and the site will pull in thousands of contractors, consultants, equipment vendors, and corporate teams from across the country.

The Syracuse Grand is a practical place to stay for that work: six miles from the campus on Caughdenoy Road, four miles from the airport, and right off Interstate 90 with easy access to downtown Syracuse, Destiny USA, and the rest of Onondaga County. Sixty-one renovated rooms, free hot breakfast, free parking, free Wi-Fi, an indoor pool and hot tub, and a 24-hour fitness center and front desk.

Driving to the Micron site

From the hotel to Caughdenoy Road.

A simple, ten-minute commute up I-481 — far easier than the drive in from downtown Syracuse during morning rush hour.

The route

  • From the hotel at 136 Transistor Pkwy, head east to Electronics Pkwy and merge onto I-90 / I-481.
  • Take I-481 north toward Watertown / Cicero.
  • Exit at NY-31 (exit 8) toward Cicero / Bridgeport.
  • Follow signs to Caughdenoy Road and the Micron site.

Allow 10–15 minutes in typical traffic. The route avoids most downtown congestion and stays on highway grade most of the way.
