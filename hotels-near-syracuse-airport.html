A short drive to the gate

Built for early flights and late arrivals.

Syracuse Hancock International Airport (SYR) is a small, easy regional hub — TSA wait times are usually short, gates are close together, and the drive in from our hotel is about seven minutes via I-81. That makes us a sensible pick if you're catching a 6 a.m. flight, recovering from a late-night arrival, or just need somewhere reliable for a one-night layover.

The Syracuse Grand is open 24 hours. Late check-ins and early departures are normal here — so are crew stays, parents picking up students from Syracuse University, and businesspeople flying in for a meeting at Lockheed Martin (one mile away) or the Micron site (six miles north). We have plenty of free on-site parking, hot breakfast every morning, and an indoor pool and hot tub if you have time before your flight.

Need a quiet room, a king with a workspace, or a two-queen for a family detour? Our rooms page shows the full lineup.

From the airport to the hotel

Driving directions and tips.

From SYR terminal to 136 Transistor Pkwy

  • Exit the airport via Col. Eileen Collins Blvd toward I-81 South.
  • Take I-81 South to I-90 West (NY State Thruway).
  • Exit at Electronics Pkwy / Liverpool (exit 37).
  • Turn onto Transistor Pkwy — we're on the left.
  • Total: about 4 miles, 7 minutes in normal traffic.

Rideshare and taxi

Uber and Lyft both serve SYR with a designated pickup zone. Expect $15–$25 from the airport to the hotel, depending on time of day. A short ride for a fair price.

Park-and-fly & long-term parking

Park-and-fly arrangements may be available — call (315) 701-4400 for current options, rates, and shuttle availability. We have plenty of well-lit on-site parking.

Other useful airport-area facts

  • SYR airlines: American, Delta, United, JetBlue, Allegiant, Frontier, and Breeze fly out of Syracuse Hancock with non-stops to most major US hubs.
  • TSA: Security usually moves quickly, but check the SYR website for current wait times before you leave.
  • Coffee & food: Our hot breakfast starts early. If you're catching a sunrise flight and miss it, there are coffee shops at the terminal.
