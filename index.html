Welcome

An effortless place to land in Central New York.

Conveniently set off Interstate 90, the Syracuse Grand is a 100% smoke‑free, pet‑friendly hotel built around what really matters when you travel — comfortable rooms, a warm welcome at the front desk, a great breakfast, and quick access to everywhere you need to be. Whether you're here for a game, a concert at Empower Federal Credit Union Amphitheater, a layover at Hancock International, or a long weekend at Destiny USA, you'll feel at home from the moment you arrive.
