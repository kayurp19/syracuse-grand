Welcome

An effortless place to land in Central New York.

Conveniently set off Interstate 90, the Syracuse Grand is a 100% smoke‑free, pet‑friendly hotel built around what really matters when you travel — comfortable rooms, a warm welcome at the front desk, a great breakfast, and quick access to everywhere you need to be. Whether you're here for a meeting at Lockheed Martin Electronics Park next door, a concert at Empower FCU Amphitheater at Lakeview, a layover at Syracuse Hancock International Airport, or a long weekend at Destiny USA, you'll feel at home from the moment you arrive.

The Location

A short drive to everything Syracuse has to offer.

From Destiny USA to the New York State Fairgrounds, from Syracuse University to Onondaga Lake Park, from Lockheed Martin to the future Micron campus — the Syracuse Grand puts Central New York's biggest moments minutes from your door.

  • Walking distance to Lockheed Martin Electronics Park
  • 4 miles to Syracuse Hancock International Airport (SYR)
  • 10 minutes to Destiny USA & Armory Square
  • 6 miles to the Micron campus in Clay, NY
  • 7 miles to the New York State Fairgrounds
  • Easy access from I‑90 (NYS Thruway)
