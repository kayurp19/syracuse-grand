Pet owners — we get it

A real pet-friendly hotel, not just a 'pets allowed' sign.

A lot of Syracuse-area hotels say they're pet-friendly. Most aren't really set up for it. The Syracuse Grand actually thought through what makes a stay work with a dog — designated grass walking areas right on property (not a parking lot), pet-friendly rooms on a specific floor (so guests with allergies aren't randomly assigned to a previous pet room), and front desk staff who keep treats behind the counter.

And what makes us special: we're five minutes from Onondaga Lake Park, which has a seven-mile paved loop trail and a dedicated off-leash dog area. After a long road trip, your dog gets a real walk — not a quick hotel-parking-lot break.

Pet policy basics: Dogs under 50 lbs welcome, $25/night fee per stay (not per pet, per night), maximum 2 dogs per room, must be leashed in common areas, and please don't leave pets unattended in rooms. Call (315) 701-4400 or use our contact form with any specific pet questions.

Walking your dog in Syracuse

Where the dogs go around here.

We've sent a lot of guests to a lot of trails. Here's what we recommend.

On-property options

  • Grass perimeter areas: Designated dog-walking spots along the property edge, away from the main entrance. Waste bag stations on-site.
  • Side and back walkways: Quiet for early-morning or late-night bathroom breaks.

Best dog walks within 10 minutes of the hotel

  • Onondaga Lake Park: Five minutes from the hotel. Seven-mile paved loop trail along the lake. Dedicated off-leash dog area at the West Shore. Bathrooms, water fountains, and parking — and one of the most beautiful walks in Central New York.
  • Long Branch Park: Eight minutes from the hotel. Dog-friendly walking trails, river views, picnic areas.
  • Onondaga Lake Loop (the longer version): If you want a real workout, the full perimeter loop is closer to 12 miles.

Dog-friendly stops in town

  • Wagner's Farm Market: Outdoor seating area where dogs are welcome. Great farm produce, ice cream, and a casual scene.
  • Heid's of Liverpool: Outdoor walk-up window, dogs welcome on the patio.
  • Wegmans Liverpool (outdoor patio): Some Wegmans locations welcome leashed dogs at outdoor seating — call ahead.

Veterinary and pet services in case you need them

  • Liverpool Village Animal Hospital: 4 minutes from the hotel — full veterinary services.
  • Veterinary Medical Center of CNY (24-hour emergency): 12 minutes from the hotel — for after-hours emergencies.
  • Petco / PetSmart on Route 31: 8 minutes — supplies, grooming, leash replacements.

FAQ

Common questions about traveling with pets.

Is Syracuse Grand pet-friendly?

Yes. Well-behaved dogs under 50 lbs are welcome, with a $25/night pet fee. Maximum 2 dogs per room.

What's the pet fee at Syracuse Grand?

$25 per night per stay (not per pet). Pet fee covers extra cleaning and is non-refundable.

Are there dog-friendly walking areas near the hotel?

Yes — designated grass areas right on property, plus Onondaga Lake Park (5 min away) with 7 miles of paved trail and a dedicated off-leash dog area.

Are cats allowed?

We're primarily set up for dogs. Cats may be permitted on a case-by-case basis — please call (315) 701-4400 in advance.

Can I leave my pet alone in the room?

We ask that pets not be left unattended. If you need to step away, please make arrangements with a dog walker or doggy daycare. Our front desk can recommend trusted local options.

Are service animals welcome?

Yes — service animals are always welcome, with no pet fee. Emotional support animals are subject to standard pet policy.
