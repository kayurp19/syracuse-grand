Neighborhoods

Where Syracuse actually lives.

Syracuse is a city of villages — sixteen of them, by Visit Syracuse's count. Here are the ones you'll want to actually visit during your stay, and what each is known for.

Liverpool

Our village

On the eastern shore of Onondaga Lake. 8+ miles of trail, classic Heid's hot dogs, Limp Lizard BBQ, and the Salt Museum. The Syracuse Grand sits a few minutes from the village center.

Armory Square

Downtown nightlife

The lively heart of downtown Syracuse — restored industrial buildings now packed with restaurants, bars, boutiques, and clubs. The Shot Clock Monument and Onondaga Creekwalk are here.

Skaneateles

The Finger Lakes jewel

A 25-minute drive west. Skaneateles Lake is famously the cleanest in the country, and the village was named to O Magazine's Charming American Towns list. Boutiques, the Sherwood Inn, Doug's Fish Fry.

Tipperary Hill

The Irish neighborhood

Famous for the only "green-on-top" traffic light in America. Coleman's Authentic Irish Pub anchors a tight-knit West Side neighborhood. Best on St. Patrick's Day.

University Hill

Syracuse University

SU, SUNY ESF, and SUNY Upstate Medical share this hill. 19th-century architecture, college bars, the Carrier Dome, and a great walk during fall foliage and SU Family Weekend.

Inner Harbor

Lakefront revival

An up-and-coming district built on the former Barge Canal terminal. Walk the harbor, grab a beer at Meiers Creek Inner Harbor, eat lakefront. Walking distance once you arrive.

Westcott

Funky & eclectic

"#WestcottNation" — coffee shops, bakeries, fresh restaurants, and the annual Westcott Street Cultural Fair. Independent and proud of it.

Cazenovia

Lake-village charm

Twenty-five minutes southeast on Cazenovia Lake. The Brewster Inn and Brae Loch Inn for fine dining, Owera Vineyards and Meiers Creek Brewery for tastings, Critz Farms for u-pick.

Things to Do

The signature attractions.

The places that show up on every Syracuse itinerary — for good reason. These are within a short drive of our hotel.

  • Onondaga Lake ParkThe "Central Park of Central New York." 8+ miles of trail, Wegmans Playground, the 16,000 sq ft skatepark, and the Salt Museum on an original salt-boiling block. 4 minutes from the Syracuse Grand.
  • Destiny USA2.4 million sq ft of shopping, dining, and entertainment. 250+ stores, antique carousel, WonderWorks, Margaritaville, Texas de Brazil, Apple, and the Cheesecake Factory.
  • JMA Wireless DomeHome of Syracuse Orange football, lacrosse, and basketball — and stadium concerts (Springsteen, Billy Joel). 49,000+ for football, 34,000+ for basketball.
  • Rosamond Gifford Zoo700+ animals including endangered Asian elephants, Amur tigers, snow leopards, and a giant Pacific octopus. The Honeybee Café onsite makes it a half-day.
  • Erie Canal MuseumHoused in the 1850 Weighlock Building — the last of its kind. Full-size replica canal boat and the story of how the canal built Syracuse. Free.
  • Everson Museum of Art10,000+ works of American painting, sculpture, video, and the world's most important ceramics collection. The I.M. Pei building is itself worth the visit.
  • Landmark Theatre1928 Art Deco palace on the National Register of Historic Places. Broadway tours, comedy headliners, concerts. The interior alone is worth a ticket.
  • NBT Bank StadiumHome of the Syracuse Mets — the AAA affiliate of the New York Mets. 11,117 seats, cheap tickets, and a great summer-evening plan.
  • St. Joseph's Health Amphitheater at Lakeview17,500-seat outdoor amphitheater on Onondaga Lake. Big-name country, rock, and rap shows all summer.
  • Beak & Skiff Apple Orchards20 minutes south. Pick-your-own apples, wagon rides, donuts and cider. The 1911 Tasting Room serves their hard cider and spirits.
  • Green Lakes State ParkTwenty minutes east. Two glacial lakes with that famous turquoise color, hiking, swimming beach, and a championship golf course.
  • The OncenterThree city blocks of convention space, the War Memorial Arena (1951), and the Civic Center theaters. Hosts hockey, Disney on Ice, conventions.

Food & Drink

Where to eat in Syracuse.

The classics, the date-night picks, and the places only locals know. For a deeper Liverpool-specific list, see the Liverpool dining guide.

Iconic

Heid's of Liverpool

A 105-year-old hot dog stand on Oswego Street. Hofmann franks, German potato salad, root beer floats. The Syracuse-area institution.

Iconic

Pastabilities

Armory Square. Handmade pasta, the legendary spicy hot tomato oil, the famous stretch bread. Always busy — go early or be patient.

Date Night

The Craftsman Wood Grille & Tap House

Cozy by the outdoor fireplace, craft cocktails, wood-fired entrées. A favorite for an unhurried evening.

Date Night

Rosalie's Cucina

Skaneateles. A quaint Italian kitchen and bakery — house-made pastas, Tuscan-style dining, fresh-baked everything.

BBQ

Limp Lizard BBQ

Liverpool. Smoked brisket, ribs, and pulled pork in a relaxed bar setting. The mac & cheese is a love letter.

Brunch

Mother's Cupboard

Famous for the Frittata Challenge — a 5-pound omelet. Even if you're not attempting it, the breakfast is exceptional.

Coffee

Recess Coffee

Locally roasted, friendly baristas, room for laptops. A favorite stop on the way to a meeting.

Sweet

Sweet on Chocolate

Skaneateles. Handcrafted truffles, chocolate-covered potato chips, beautiful gift packaging. Bring some home.

Café

Skaneateles Bakery

Pastries and a classic breakfast/lunch menu in the heart of the lake village. The way to start a Skaneateles morning.

Breweries

Onondaga County's beer scene.

From Syracuse's oldest working brewery to farmhouse taprooms a short drive out, here's where to find a great pint.

  • Middle Ages Brewing Co.The oldest working brewery in Syracuse. Started in British-style ales, now expanded to New England and Brut IPAs. Spacious tasting room.
  • Talking Cursive BrewingFemale and veteran-owned, in the heart of Armory Square. Staff trained in the Cicerone program — the sommelier equivalent for beer.
  • Buried Acorn BrewingTwo locations. Mixed-fermentation specialists known for sour and funky beers, plus a growing barrel-aged collection.
  • Meiers Creek (Cazenovia)20-minute drive out. Spacious indoor and outdoor seating, lawn games, heated lamps, live events. Worth the trip.
  • Meiers Creek Inner HarborSame brewery, urban location. Beer, cider, seltzer, and small plates minutes outside downtown.
  • Heritage Hill FarmhouseKid- and dog-friendly with a playground and animals on site. Sunset views, game-day-friendly. A second North Syracuse location opened 2024.
  • Local 315 BrewingAn actual working farm — chickens, goats, a hop field. Live music, big outdoor seating, IPAs and fruit-infused beers.
  • Eastwood BrewingCozy neighborhood brewery showing Orange pride. Known for stouts and the C4 IPA.
  • Skaneateles BreweryOut in the lake village. Attached to Last Shot Distillery — pair the visit.
  • Willow Rock BrewingSouth of SU campus. Energetic tasting room, rotating crafts, includes the revival of the iconic Congress brand.
  • Empire BrewingArmory Square cornerstone since 1994. Brews on-site and at a farm brewery in Cazenovia.
  • Syracuse Suds FactoryThe first brewery in Syracuse since the closing of Haberle Congress in the 1960s. Prominent corner spot in Armory Square.

Day Trips

Easy escapes from Syracuse.

All within an hour or so. Pick one for a half-day or string a few together.

25 min

Skaneateles Lake

The "Jewel of the Finger Lakes." Stroll the village, lunch at Doug's Fish Fry, wine and views at Anyela's Vineyards. A perfect weekend turn.

20 min

Green Lakes State Park

Two glacial lakes with otherworldly turquoise color, plus hiking trails, a swimming beach, and a championship golf course.

25 min

Cazenovia

Lakeside village dining at the Brewster Inn, wine at Owera Vineyards, beer at Meiers Creek, u-pick fruit and a kids' area at Critz Farms.

35 min

Beak & Skiff Apple Orchards

U-pick apples, wagon rides, hot apple fritters, donuts, and the 1911 Tasting Room for hard cider and spirits. Best in fall.

40 min

Tinker Falls & Labrador Hollow

A peaceful hike to a tiered waterfall and a unique boardwalk through wetlands. Spring through fall, easy difficulty.

20 min

Pratt's Falls State Park

137-foot glacier-formed waterfall plus the site of Onondaga County's first mill (1796). Trails, picnic spots, and an archery range.

45 min

Beaver Lake Nature Center

Family-friendly trails, guided nature programs, and a great kids' option for a half-day outdoors.

75 min

Finger Lakes wine country

Cayuga and Seneca Lake wine trails are an easy drive. Make it a long lunch or a full day; the Riesling country is world-class.

Seasonal

What's good when you're here.

Syracuse changes character with the seasons. Here's what to time your visit around.

Spring · Mar–May

St. Patrick's Day & opening day

Tipperary Hill goes all-in for St. Paddy's. Syracuse Mets opening day at NBT Bank Stadium. Onondaga Lake Park reopens to full activity. Cherry blossoms at Onondaga Park.

Summer · Jun–Aug

Lake season & concerts

Onondaga Lake Park trails, Skaneateles boating, Mets baseball, and the St. Joseph's Health Amphitheater concert run. Westcott Street Cultural Fair late summer.

Late Summer

The Great New York State Fair

13 days starting late August at the State Fairgrounds, ending Labor Day. The largest agricultural fair in the Northeast. Plan parking in advance.

Fall · Sep–Nov

Foliage & apple season

Syracuse fall is exceptional. Beak & Skiff for apple picking, Lafayette Apple Festival in October, drives through the Route 20 Scenic Byway. SU football season is here too.

Winter · Dec–Feb

Snow, ski, & SU basketball

Labrador Mountain and Song Mountain for skiing, Highland Forest for cross-country. SU basketball is in season at the Dome. Winterfest in Liverpool every February.

Year-Round

The Dome

JMA Wireless Dome (the "Loud House") draws Springsteen and Billy Joel as readily as it draws SU games. Always check the calendar — there's usually something on.

Business & Employers

Major Syracuse-area employers & institutions.

Visiting a client, candidate, family member, or campus? Here are the major employers, hospitals, universities, and corporate campuses in the Syracuse and Onondaga County area — with approximate drive times from the Syracuse Grand in Liverpool.

Healthcare & hospitals

  • Upstate University Hospital & SUNY Upstate Medical UniversityThe region's largest employer with 11,000+ staff. Hospital, medical school, and Golisano Children's Hospital on University Hill. ~12 minutes from the hotel.
  • St. Joseph's Health HospitalMajor Catholic-affiliated health system with 4,800+ employees. Main campus at 301 Prospect Ave. ~10 minutes from the Syracuse Grand.
  • Crouse Health (Crouse Hospital)3,200+ employees. Independent hospital on University Hill, known for maternity and the Crouse Health Network. ~12 minutes away.
  • VA Medical Center SyracuseDepartment of Veterans Affairs hospital on Irving Avenue, on the University Hill campus cluster. ~12 minutes.
  • Community Memorial Hospital (Hamilton)Affiliated rural hospital ~45 minutes south. Frequent referral and consultant traffic to Syracuse.

Technology & manufacturing

  • Micron Technology (Clay, NY)The transformative $100 billion semiconductor megafab under construction in Clay — projected to employ 9,000 people directly plus tens of thousands in the supply chain. ~10 minutes from the Syracuse Grand. Visiting Micron contractors and engineers stay with us regularly.
  • Lockheed Martin Mission Systems & Training4,100+ employees at Electronics Park in Salina. Defense radar, sonar, and aerospace systems. ~6 minutes from the hotel.
  • SRC Inc. (Cicero)1,000+ employees in Cicero. R&D for defense, environment, and intelligence. Founded 1957 by Syracuse University. ~10 minutes away.
  • Saab Defense and Security USADefense electronics and radar at Electronics Park, alongside Lockheed Martin. ~6 minutes from the Syracuse Grand.
  • Anheuser-Busch Brewery (Baldwinsville)The largest brewery in New York State. ~20 minutes west of the hotel. Closed to general public, but a frequent destination for vendors and contractors.
  • Bristol-Myers Squibb (East Syracuse)Pharmaceutical manufacturing facility. ~15 minutes east of the hotel.
  • Welch Allyn / Hillrom (Skaneateles Falls)Medical device manufacturing, now part of Baxter. ~30 minutes from the Syracuse Grand.
  • Carrier Corporation (DeWitt)HVAC pioneer, founded in Syracuse. Engineering and operations remain in DeWitt. ~15 minutes away.

Universities & education

  • Syracuse University4,700+ employees, 22,000+ students. Whitman School of Management, Newhouse School, Maxwell School, College of Engineering & Computer Science. ~15 minutes from the hotel.
  • SUNY ESF (College of Environmental Science and Forestry)Adjacent to SU on University Hill. Forestry, environmental science, sustainability. ~15 minutes away.
  • SUNY Upstate Medical UniversityMedical, nursing, and health professions. The region's largest employer (with the hospital). ~12 minutes.
  • Le Moyne CollegeJesuit liberal arts college in DeWitt. ~15 minutes east of the hotel.
  • Onondaga Community College (OCC)Two-year campus on Onondaga Hill, just south of downtown. ~15 minutes from the Syracuse Grand.
  • Syracuse City School DistrictOne of the largest public-sector employers in Onondaga County. Frequent host of conferences and recruiting visits.

Government, finance & energy

  • National Grid (Syracuse)3,000+ employees. Electric and gas utility serving Upstate NY. Major operations center in Syracuse. ~12 minutes.
  • Onondaga County (Civic Center)County government offices in downtown Syracuse. Hosts conferences, public meetings, and the John H. Mulroy Civic Center. ~15 minutes.
  • City of Syracuse (City Hall)Municipal government in downtown. ~15 minutes from the hotel.
  • NBT BankRegional bank, naming sponsor of NBT Bank Stadium. Major commercial banking presence in Onondaga County.
  • M&T Bank, KeyBank, Community Bank N.A.Major regional banking offices throughout Syracuse and Onondaga County.
  • Carrols Restaurant GroupHeadquartered in Syracuse — one of the largest Burger King franchisees in the country. ~12 minutes from the hotel.

Retail & corporate centers

  • Wegmans Food Markets5,200+ regional employees. Multiple stores throughout Onondaga County, plus Wegmans Landing at Onondaga Lake Park. ~5 minutes to the nearest store.
  • Destiny USA2.4 million sq ft — the sixth-largest shopping mall in the U.S. 250+ stores, restaurants, and entertainment venues. ~10 minutes from the Syracuse Grand.
  • Walmart (Onondaga County)4,600+ regional employees across multiple stores including Cicero, Camillus, and Clay supercenters.
  • Price Chopper / Market 32 (Northeast Grocery)6,600+ regional employees. Major grocery chain with stores throughout the area.
  • The Oncenter Convention ComplexThree city blocks of meeting, convention, and banquet space. War Memorial Arena, Mulroy Civic Center Theaters, Pirro Convention Center. ~15 minutes.
  • NYS Fairgrounds (Geddes)The Great New York State Fair venue, plus year-round events, expos, and conventions. ~10 minutes from the hotel.

Transportation & logistics

  • Syracuse Hancock International Airport (SYR)The region's main airport, 4 miles from the Syracuse Grand. American, Delta, JetBlue, Southwest, United, Allegiant, Frontier all serve SYR. ~8 minutes from the hotel.
  • CSX TransportationMajor Class I freight rail operations through Syracuse — a key Northeast hub.
  • FedEx Ground & UPS hubsRegional sorting and distribution facilities serving Central New York.
  • NYS Thruway AuthorityI-90 corridor through Syracuse. Major regional headquarters and operations.
  • Centro (CNY Regional Transportation Authority)Public transit operator for Onondaga, Cayuga, Oneida, and Oswego counties.
